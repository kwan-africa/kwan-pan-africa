### **1. Project Idea (Word Count: 184 / Max 200 Words)**
Over 90% of West Africa’s tourism economy operates informally. The best boutique eco-lodges, grassroots cultural guides, street food artisans, and trotro transit routes are not listed on Expedia or Airbnb—they rely entirely on Instagram DMs and WhatsApp. Foreign travelers face severe friction paying local hosts who lack formal bank accounts, while local operators risk non-payment.

**Kwan AI** is a Pan-African travel concierge platform that digitizes West Africa’s informal real economy. Using pgvector RAG AI matching, Kwan composes personalized multi-day itineraries tailored to traveler budgets and energy styles (Action-Packed, Relaxed, Balanced). 

To eliminate fraud, foreign travelers pay via Paystack using international Credit Cards or Apple Pay. Funds are held in **Kwan Paystack Escrow** and automatically released 24 hours post-tour directly into the host’s **Mobile Money (MTN MoMo, Telecel Cash)** wallet—with zero bank account required. Kwan includes an interactive 4-step national ID verification wizard (Ghana Card/NIN) for local hosts, real-time trotro station route maps with hand-signal guides, and an AI dialect pronunciation assistant for Twi, Yoruba, and Pidgin.
